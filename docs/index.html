BEHIND THE SHELF

A thoughtful little prototype.

Discover is a standalone midterm project developing the book-discovery aspect of Bookaholix, a separate app in development. It helps readers rediscover books from their own uploaded library.

What powers it

Discover searches only the books in your imported CSV. Known title-and-author matches can reuse bundled source-backed metadata. Other books remain browseable with their imported title and author. You can add personal story classifications to improve matching.

What counts as evidence

Imported book identity, source-backed information and reader-provided labels are kept distinct. A Goodreads export usually does not include genres or tropes. Those details cannot be extracted from a title alone. Unknown details do not satisfy filters; mood is a reading impression. Use commas to separate distinct preferences: “dark, fantasy” asks for a mood and a genre, while “dark fantasy” names a genre.

About the artwork

The typographic jackets and decorative illustrations were created for this prototype using code. They are not the books’ official covers.

Your browser, your preferences

Taste preferences, search interactions, discovered book metadata and your saved wishlist stay in this browser’s local storage. Library files, notes and searches are processed locally. No automatic external book lookup or paid AI service is used in this version. Publisher links open externally only when you choose them.

Your story notes

These are your classifications, not independently verified facts. They help you find this book again. Separate multiple labels with semicolons.
